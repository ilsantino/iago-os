The RSF engagement is structured as a barter, not a commercial contract.

**Deal structure:**
- RSF funds: hardware, software/AWS, viáticos (travel if needed)
- RSF does NOT pay: salaries, fees, or any iaGO labor
- iaGO contributes: all coordination + ML + infra hours (Santiago + Fran + Sebas + Claude/Codex)
- iaGO receives in lieu of fees: (a) a working system for RSF (deliverable), (b) a case study iaGO uses for portfolio + future client pitches, (c) likely a co-authored paper or HuggingFace dataset to Santiago's name + RSF as co-author

**Why:** RSF's top guy explicitly framed this as helping Santiago "robustecer mi perfil" before the second UC3M attempt (Oct 2026). The PoC IS the perfil-strengthening artifact. iaGO has no current enterprise credibility to charge fees against; the case study is the path to building it.

**How to apply:**
- When selecting which of the 22 RSF candidate pilots to pitch, optimize for: (1) clean case-study narrative, (2) publishable research artifact (paper or HF dataset), (3) AWS portfolio depth, (4) operational impact for RSF that justifies them paying costs
- DO NOT optimize for commercial fee maximization — there are no fees
- Items with HIGH research-bonus weight (e.g., #03 pest detection / first LATAM CEA dataset, #14 shelf-life, #01 climate-RL surrogate paper) move UP the ranking under this frame vs the commercial-fee frame
- Items with strong commercial impact but zero research artifact (e.g., #04 yield forecast, #05 fruit grading, #10 carbon LCA) become weaker standalone picks because they don't carry the paper/dataset compensation, but can serve as visible-deliverable companions to a research-strong item
- Hybrid pilots that combine a research-strong item with a visible-deliverable item (e.g., #03 + visible alert system; #5+#14 shared packing camera with shelf-life paper) are particularly attractive
- See also: [RSF top-dog personal relationship](project_rsf_relationship.md)
