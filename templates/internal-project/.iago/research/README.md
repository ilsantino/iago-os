# `research/`

One dated artefact per question: `YYYY-MM-DD-{slug}.md`. A multi-leg pass may add
a sibling directory of the same name holding its legs.

Superseded research is deleted; decision-bearing research moves to `_archive/`
with a pointer. Nothing here is polished after the fact — it is dated evidence,
and its value is that it says what was known on that day.
